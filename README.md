# 🍕 Pizza Clicker 2 — Legend of the Slice

A juicy, over-the-top pizza clicker game. Tap the pizza, build a kitchen empire, cover your pie in toppings, and rebirth your way to **Pizza Eternal**.

> 🎮 **Play it:** just open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no dependencies.

## How to play

- **Tap the pizza** to earn Pizza Gold and build combos.
- **⚙️ Upgrades tab** — hire Master Chefs, Robo Bakers, Delivery Drones, Dough Star Reactors... automation (Pizza Gold / second) builds itself.
- **🍕 Toppings tab** — buy Mushrooms, Pepperoni, Pineapple, Gold Truffles and more. Every topping *literally lands on your pizza* and gives permanent production or per-tap gains.
- **🌟 Legend tab** — rebirth the realm for **Pizza Legend Points** (+5% to everything *forever* per point), climb ranks from 🍳 Apprentice Chef to 🔥 Pizza Eternal, and spend points on permanent perks like **Slices of Power**, **Sauce of Time**, and **Dough of Fortune**.

## Features

- Real CSS-built pizza: pan crust, bubbling cheese, sauce bleed, slice cuts, rising steam, and a bobbing wooden board.
- 14 kitchen upgrades, 9 toppings, 3 legendary perks, 20 achievements, chef ranks.
- **Golden Pizza** events — click the flying 🥇 pizza for a big payout.
- Combo ring timer, particle bursts, flying cheese bits, confetti showers.
- WebAudio sound effects with a mute toggle 🔊.
- Full progress autosave in `localStorage` + a reset button.

## Tech

Single-file HTML/CSS/JS — no frameworks, no install, works offline.
